# Calculations

Every calculation Ledgr does, where it lives, what governs it, and what test holds it. For a contributor changing a figure, or a user who wants to know where a number comes from.

This describes the code as it is today. Where a decided change will alter a figure, the Gaps column says "Changes in v0.11 (decision X)". Until then the figure stays as described.

## How to read a row

| Column          | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Id**          | Stable id. Never reused or renumbered. Answer-key tests carry it in their title (`tax/B2 [TAX-05] …`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Computes**    | What the figure is, in one sentence.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Function**    | `path:symbol`, path under `src/` (or `tax-data/` for shipped figures). A bare `symbol` is in the file named before it in the same cell. A symbol may be the function that holds the step, not one named for it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Authority**   | What decides the right answer: a law, regulation or publication, with section and year; **Owner design** (Ledgr's own rule, with the doc section or decision that sets it); or **Arithmetic** (follows from the other rows, no outside answer).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Status**      | **Verified**: a written spec fetched the cited source and checked the rule or figure against it. **Unverified**: cited but not checked; treat the citation as a pointer. **Owner design** and **Arithmetic** as above. A row is Verified only when every source it leans on was fetched; one unfetched source makes it Unverified.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Checked for** | Tax-law rows only: the tax year whose law the row was last checked against. A row is a tax-law row when its Authority cites federal tax or benefit law: the IRC, Treasury regulations, an IRS publication, form, schedule, instructions, notice or revenue procedure, a public law (P.L.), the U.S. Code or CFR (Social Security, Medicare), or a CMS, SSA or HHS notice, whatever its Status. **Not checked**: no review has read that law for this row yet. "—": not a tax-law row. See [Tax law, every year](#tax-law-every-year).                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Test**        | The test files under `tests/` that hold the rule, and their kind. **Independent**: the expected value comes from the source or a derivation that doesn't run Ledgr. **Self-derived**: pinned from Ledgr's output, or hand arithmetic on Ledgr's own formula. **Bound**: an inequality or band, not a value. **None**: no test.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Tested**      | The answer key: each test file under `tests/` with answer-key cases for this row (titled `<area>/<id> [ROW-ID]`, mostly in `answer-key-*` files), and how many pass and how many are expected failures (`it.fails`, each naming the release and TODO item that fixes it). A tagged check that isn't one countable case (a title built in a loop, such as every published table cell, or one not titled `<area>/<id>`) reads **table check**, with no count. Every case's figure is worked by hand from the authority, with its working beside it. **None**: no answer-key case yet. Generated from the test titles by `pnpm docs:update` (`scripts/calculations-tested.ts`); `pnpm docs:verify` fails when it's stale. Don't edit it by hand. |
| **Gaps**        | What's known to be wrong or missing today: the one place calculation bugs are listed. A link to [RULES § Known Gaps](RULES.md#known-gaps) where it's listed there; "none known" otherwise.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

A row being Verified says the rule was checked against its source, not that the code is right. Read the Gaps column for that. A row with no answer-key case (Tested "None") has only the tests in its Test column, and a self-derived test can move with the code.

## Adding or changing a calculation

- **Every new calculation needs a row,** in the same commit: an id in its area, the function, an authority, the status, the test, and for a tax-law row the year it was checked for. A calculation with no outside authority still needs one: Owner design names the RULES, DESIGN or GLOSSARY section that defines it, and that section must exist.
- **A cited source starts Unverified.** It becomes Verified when someone fetches the document and checks the rule against it, and says which edition.
- **Changing a figure** names its source and the size of the change in dollars on a stated example (RULES § Known Gaps).
- **A test only counts as Independent** if its expected value can be worked out without running Ledgr.
- **An answer-key case** is titled `<area>/<id> [ROW-ID]`, usually in an `answer-key-*` test file (`tests/fixtures/answer-key.ts` has the helpers and rules); run `pnpm docs:update` to refresh Tested.
- **A moved function or test** gets its path fixed in the row. `pnpm docs:verify` fails when a Function or Test path doesn't exist.
- **A new export of `lib/pure/` (the engine included) needs a row naming it.** `pnpm check:calculation-register` (CI) fails an export with no row, and a new row with no risk rating (H, M or L, in `scripts/calculation-register-baseline.json`). The exports without a row today and the high-risk rows with no independent test are listed there; both lists may only shrink, so an entry that gets its row or test (or goes away) is removed in the same commit.

Figures that change each year (brackets, limits, thresholds) live in `tax-data/us-federal/<year>.json`, not in code. Rows below cite the law; the year's figure is in the data file, and its `sources` name the notice it came from.

## Tax law, every year

A new tax year needs more than new figures: deductions, credits, phase-outs, sunsets and ages change in law too. When a `tax-data/us-federal/<year>.json` is added ([tax-data/README.md § Adding a year](../tax-data/README.md#adding-a-year)):

1. Read what changed in the law for that year. For each change, find the rows whose Authority cites the section or publication it touches; fix the function, re-work that row's answer-key cases from the new law, and add cases for a new rule.
2. Set **Checked for** to the new year on every tax-law row once it's been read against that year's law.
3. Add hand-worked whole-return cases for the new year, one per supported filing status (Single, MFJ, HOH), in `tests/pure/tax/answer-key-tax.test.ts`, each titled with a `{return <year> <status>}` tag (`tax/X1 [TAX-16] {return 2026 MFJ} …`).

`tests/lint/calculations-law-year.test.ts` fails while any tax-law row is checked for an older year than the newest shipped file, or a shipped year lacks a whole-return case for a status.

## Tax

Two tax models, on purpose. The Taxes page figures this year's return from the published 1040 brackets (`calculateHouseholdTax`). The Projection prices each future year from the withholding-table shape of the same brackets, grown forward (`lib/pure/engine/tax-estimation.ts`). They should agree on the same year and income; where they can't, the row says so.
