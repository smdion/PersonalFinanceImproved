How contributions & distributions are projected
Accumulation Engine?
Routes contributions across 401k, IRA, HSA, and brokerage using waterfall, percentage, or per-account specs. Handles IRS limit growth, catch-up contributions (SECURE 2.0), employer matches, Roth/Traditional splits, and overflow to brokerage.
Full methodology →
Decumulation Engine?
Computes annual need (budget × inflation − Social Security from each person's start age), grosses up for taxes, and routes withdrawals via bracket filling (Traditional to bracket cap, then whichever of Roth or Brokerage is cheaper that year, HSA last). Enforces RMDs, optional Roth conversions, 8 dynamic spending strategies (Morningstar), and IRMAA/ACA cliff awareness.
Full methodology →
